TITLE: RBI Warning on Fictitious Offers of Lottery Winnings and Cheap Funds
SOURCE: Reserve Bank of India (RBI)
CONTENT: The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has repeatedly warned the public against falling prey to fictitious offers of huge lottery winnings, cheap funds, or international prize money. Fraudsters often send letters, emails, or call victims claiming they have won a lottery (such as KBC lottery draws) or that an unclaimed inheritance is waiting for them. The victim is then asked to deposit an "upfront processing fee", "transfer tax", or "clearance charge" into a specific bank account. The RBI states that it never stores money for individuals, does not run any lucky draws, and never requests processing fees to release winnings. Any demand for prepayment to receive funds is a scam.

TITLE: RBI Directive on KYC Update Phishing and Account Suspension Threats
SOURCE: Reserve Bank of India (RBI)
CONTENT: The RBI has issued guidelines to caution bank customers against fake calls, messages, or emails threatening immediate suspension or blocking of their bank accounts, debit cards, or digital wallets. In these scams, callers pose as bank managers, executives, or RBI officers and tell the victim that their KYC (Know Your Customer) has expired. They create panic and urgency, demanding that the victim perform KYC updates over the phone or click a link. The RBI reiterates that commercial banks and financial institutions never request sensitive credentials—including One-Time Passwords (OTPs), PINs, CVV codes, or passwords—via phone calls or text messages. Any call demanding an OTP to verify identity or stop account blocking is fraudulent.

TITLE: RBI Caution on Unauthorized Digital Lending and Loan Apps
SOURCE: Reserve Bank of India (RBI)
CONTENT: The RBI warns members of the public against signing up for unauthorized digital lending apps that promise instant, paperless loans without credit checks. These platforms often charge usurious interest rates, hidden processing fees, and employ illegal, aggressive recovery tactics. Some callers masquerade as recovery agents or bank representatives, requesting bank transfers, processing fees, or threat-based repayments. The RBI advises customers to verify that any lending service is registered as a bank or Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC) before sharing personal documents or making transfers.

TITLE: SEBI & RBI Advisory on Fake Stock Market Trading Apps, Mutual Fund Tips & Guaranteed IPO Allotment Scams
SOURCE: Reserve Bank of India (RBI) / SEBI
CONTENT: RBI and SEBI warn investors against fraudsters creating fake stock trading apps, cloned mutual fund investment portals, and WhatsApp/Telegram stock tip groups. Scammers promise guaranteed daily stock returns of 10% to 50%, exclusive pre-IPO allotments, or insider mutual fund tips. They trick investors into depositing funds into personal or shell company bank accounts instead of SEBI-registered brokers. Investors are advised never to transfer money for stock market trading or mutual funds to personal accounts or unverified trading applications.

TITLE: IRDAI & RBI Caution on Unclaimed Insurance Policy Bonus and Lapsed Policy Maturity Refunds
SOURCE: Reserve Bank of India (RBI) / IRDAI
CONTENT: RBI and IRDAI issue a public warning against callers posing as insurance company executives or IRDAI officers claiming that an old or lapsed insurance policy has accrued an unclaimed bonus of lakhs of rupees. The scammer tells the victim that to release the accumulated bonus or maturity amount, they must pay a mandatory "processing tax", "GST clearance fee", or purchase a new policy. IRDAI clarifies that it does not hold individual policy funds, never calls policyholders to distribute bonuses, and never collects fees to release maturity claims.

TITLE: RBI & FIU-IND Caution on Unauthorized Cryptocurrency Exchanges and Crypto Mining Pool Schemes
SOURCE: Reserve Bank of India (RBI) / FIU-IND
CONTENT: The RBI and Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU-IND) warn the public against investing in illegal cryptocurrency trading platforms, bogus bitcoin mining pools, and high-yield digital asset schemes. Fraudsters lure victims via Instagram or Telegram ads showing fake profit screenshots and demand crypto deposits or bank transfers. Once funds are deposited, the platform freezes withdrawals and demands a "tax clearance fee" to unlock funds. Citizens are advised that crypto assets carry extreme financial risk and unauthorized platforms are used for money laundering and fraud.

TITLE: EPFO & RBI Advisory on Fake Provident Fund (PF) Withdrawal Assistance and Pension Fees
SOURCE: Reserve Bank of India (RBI) / EPFO
CONTENT: RBI and EPFO caution employees against fraudsters offering illegal assistance for instant Provident Fund (PF) withdrawal, pension clearance, or UAN unblocking. Scammers demand processing fees via UPI or request Universal Account Number (UAN) login credentials and OTPs. EPFO confirms that PF claims can only be filed through the official Member Sewa Portal (unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in) and no fees are required for online claim processing.

TITLE: RBI Caution on Credit Card Limit Enhancement, Reward Points & Zero-Percent EMI Fraud
SOURCE: Reserve Bank of India (RBI)
CONTENT: The RBI warns credit cardholders against fake calls offering free credit card limit increases, zero-cost EMI conversions, or instant cashback on reward points. Scammers direct victims to fake banking web forms that capture credit card numbers, expiry dates, CVV codes, and One-Time Passwords (OTPs). The RBI reiterates that banks never request CVV codes or OTPs to upgrade credit limits or convert purchases to EMIs.
