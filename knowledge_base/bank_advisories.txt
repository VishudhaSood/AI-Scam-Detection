TITLE: SBI Safety Advisory on Fake YONO Update Links and Reward Points APK Scams
SOURCE: State Bank of India (SBI)
CONTENT: State Bank of India (SBI) cautions all customers against fraudulent SMS messages claiming that their YONO account will be blocked unless they update their PAN card or click an unverified APK link. TITLE: ICICI Bank Advisory on SIM Swap and E-SIM Fraud Operations
SOURCE: ICICI Bank
CONTENT: ICICI Bank issues a high-priority warning regarding SIM Swap and E-SIM activation fraud. Attackers contact victims pretending to be telecom customer service agents (from Jio, Airtel, or Vi) offering 5G upgrades or improved network quality. They trick the victim into sharing an eSIM registration QR code or forwarding a specific SMS (e.g. SIM2SWAP) to telecom operator numbers. ICICI Bank advises customers never to share eSIM QR codes or forward unverified telecom SMS codes.

TITLE: Bank of Baroda Security Advisory on QR Code Scanner Scams and Receive-Money Frauds
SOURCE: Bank of Baroda
CONTENT: Bank of Baroda warns customers buying or selling products online (via platforms like OLX or Quikr) about QR code frauds. Scammers pretend to be buyers (often posing as army officers or government personnel) and send a QR code to the victim claiming it is to "transfer payment". They instruct the victim to scan the QR code and enter their UPI PIN to "receive money". Bank of Baroda emphasizes a fundamental rule of UPI banking: You NEVER need to scan a QR code or enter your UPI PIN to RECEIVE money. Entering your UPI PIN always DEDUCTS money from your bank account.
